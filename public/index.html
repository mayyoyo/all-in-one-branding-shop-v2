Questions, answered

A little clarity before you order.

Are the prices shown final?

No. Prices shown are starting estimates and may change after material, artwork, finishing, quantity and delivery requirements are reviewed. Final production prices should be checked against material costs, print time, waste, design labor, packaging, profit and applicable tax.

Can I use my own design?

Yes. Use the artwork upload field in the online order form. Production files are reviewed for size, resolution, bleed and print readiness before proof approval.

How is shipping calculated?

Shipping amounts shown are estimates. Final shipping is confirmed with the order details before production. The live website should request a real carrier rate based on destination ZIP code, package size and weight, and shipping speed.

Can I order a custom quantity or size?

Yes. Select custom quantity and leave details in the notes. Non-standard sizes, multiple label designs, specialty materials and complex cuts may require a custom quote.

Will you print before I approve the design?

No. When a proof is part of your project, production starts only after you approve the proof in writing. Please check spelling, phone numbers, colors, sizes and all final details carefully.

How do I check my order status?

After checkout, save your order number and use the Track Order link with the same email address used at checkout. Status updates are provided as the order moves through production.

Can you guarantee exact screen-to-print colors?

Screen colors can differ from printed colors because displays, inks, materials and print processes vary. We can discuss material samples or a paid test print for color-sensitive projects.

What if my order arrives damaged or has a production issue?

Contact us promptly with your order number and clear photos of the issue. We review concerns under the shop's published reprint and refund policy and applicable law.
